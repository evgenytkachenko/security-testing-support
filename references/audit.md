# Customer and audit support

Make a requirements-to-evidence matrix: exact customer question/control, applicable product/version, test or practice, dated evidence reference, known limitations, accountable reviewer and response status. Distinguish implemented practice, tested control, planned work and unknown evidence. An OWASP reference or scanner report does not confer certification or prove HIPAA/SOC 2/PCI compliance.

Draft responses in the user's workspace. Do not publish or send them, make contractual assurances, assign delivery dates, accept customer terms or assert compliance on the organization's behalf. Security verifies technical claims; Legal/Compliance reviews assurance language; Product confirms roadmap statements. Review recipients and disclosure classification before sharing vulnerability detail.

Suggested draft:
“Within [product/version/scope], [test/practice] was performed on [date] using [method]. Evidence: [approved reference]. [Relevant limitations/exclusions]. [Verified remediation status or pending owner decision]. This statement applies only to the stated scope.”

Use placeholders when records are missing. Do not silently convert them into positive answers. Keep exploit details, sensitive endpoints, private source, credentials and customer data out of broad audit packets; use an approved restricted evidence channel when authorized.
